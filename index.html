What is LSD?

Six rules. Most of them are about lunch.

Lunch break

Held during the lunch break, so nobody has to choose between eating and joining.

45 minutes

Long enough to get into it, short enough to get back to the bench.

Everyone in the lab

Professors, students, and postdocs. Every member is welcome, equally.

Discussion, not a lecture

Nobody just presents. Everyone shares what they know about the topic.

Bring your lunch

Eat while you talk. Food is part of the format.

Held in English

So every lab member can take part in the conversation.
